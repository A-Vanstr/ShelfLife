# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: May 1, 2026*

We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights as a user.

## What We Collect
- Account information (name, email address)
- Content you add to the app (pantry items, household names)
- Basic app usage data for analytics and subscription management
- Device type and operating system version

## How We Use Your Data
We use your data solely to provide and improve the app experience. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising purposes.

## Images
Photos taken for scanning purposes are processed immediately and deleted. They are never stored or shared.

## Payments
All payments are handled by Apple. We do not collect or store any payment information.

## Data Storage
Your data is stored on servers located in the European Union. Our subscription provider, Superwall, may process limited usage data outside the EU.

## Your Rights
You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us at support@tryshelflife.com. You can also delete your account directly from within the app.

## Children
The app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

## Changes
We may update this policy periodically. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
